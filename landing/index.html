For vendors

Vendors register in the vendor app and an admin reviews every application. You can only accept jobs after your account is approved. If anything changes, you can update your details and you will be reviewed again.

What you are asked to submit

  • Phone number (verified with a one-time code) and business name.
  • Vehicle plate number and vehicle type. You may also add the load capacity and a vehicle photo.
  • All three documents, uploaded as photos: vehicle registration, driver’s license, and business document.
  • You may add a business license number (optional).

After you submit, your status is pending until an admin approves or rejects it. Approved vendors see paid job offers with the payout amount and accept them in the app.

How payments work

The customer pays online at booking time through Paystack. That money is held — not sent to anyone yet. When the vendor completes the pickup, the held amount is released to the vendor and both sides can see the result on the receipt. Cash payment is not accepted in the app.

If something goes wrong, either side can raise a dispute on a completed or cancelled booking. An admin reviews it and decides: refund the customer, pay the vendor, or split it. Approved refunds go back through Paystack; if the vendor was already paid out, the refund is completed manually and the customer is told so in the app.

Frequently asked questions

Which areas do you serve?
Eti-Osa and Ikeja in Lagos. Bookings outside these areas are not accepted yet.
How do I log in? Is there a password?
Customers and vendors log in with their phone number and a one-time code sent to that number. There is no password to remember.
When exactly am I charged?
When you book. You approve the full price up front, the money is held, and it is released to the vendor only after the job is completed.
Can I pay cash to the driver?
No. The app blocks cash payments — every booking must be paid online when you book.
What must vendors submit to join?
Business name, vehicle plate number and type, plus photos of three documents: vehicle registration, driver’s license, and business document. An admin reviews the application before the vendor can accept jobs.
What if something goes wrong with my pickup?
Raise a dispute from the app on the completed or cancelled booking. An admin reviews it and can refund you, pay the vendor, or split the amount — and you can see the outcome in the app.
